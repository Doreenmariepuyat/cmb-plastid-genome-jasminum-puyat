# Plastid Genome Visualization – Jasminum nudiflorum

**Name:** Puyat, Doreen Marie G.

## Genome Information

- **Scientific name:** *Jasminum nudiflorum*
- **Common name:** Winter jasmine
- **Organelle:** Chloroplast
- **NCBI Accession:** NC_008407.1
- **Genome length:** 165,121 bp
- **Genome topology:** Circular
- **Data source:** NCBI Nucleotide
- **GenBank file:** `data/Jasminum_nudiflorum_NC_008407.1.gb`

## Objective

The objective of this activity was to visualize the structure and organization of the *Jasminum nudiflorum* chloroplast genome using OrganellarGenomeDRAW (OGDRAW). The visualization was used to examine the major structural regions, gene organization, transcription direction, and GC-content pattern of the plastid genome.

## Visualization Tool

The chloroplast genome was visualized using **OrganellarGenomeDRAW (OGDRAW)**.

OGDRAW settings used:

- **Mode:** Standard
- **Sequence source:** Plastid
- **Genome structure:** Circular
- **Inverted repeat detection:** Automatic
- **GC content graph:** Enabled
- **Direction of transcription:** Enabled
- **Full legend:** Enabled
- **Intron-containing genes:** Labeled with an asterisk when available
- **Output format:** PNG

## Genome Structure

The *Jasminum nudiflorum* chloroplast genome is a circular genome with a total length of 165,121 bp. The graphical map shows the major plastid genome regions, including the Large Single-Copy (LSC) region, Small Single-Copy (SSC) region, and two Inverted Repeat regions (IRa and IRb).

The map also displays protein-coding genes, transfer RNA (tRNA) genes, ribosomal RNA (rRNA) genes, and other annotated features. Arrows indicate the direction of transcription, while the inner GC-content graph shows the distribution of GC content across the genome.
